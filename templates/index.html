Methodology

From pixels to insight.

A two-stage pipeline keeps detection broad and classification specific.

01 / INPUT

Capture

An image or video frame enters the analysis pipeline through the upload workspace.

02 / DETECT

Locate

YOLOv8 scans the scene and draws a bounding box around every detected vehicle.

03 / CLASSIFY

Identify

Each crop is normalized and passed to the trained classifier for vehicle type prediction.

04 / REPORT

Understand

Confidence scores, positions, crops, and annotated media become one readable result.
